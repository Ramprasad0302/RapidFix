# RapidFix franchises — how it works

**Head office (Super Admin / Admin)** sees everything. **Franchise managers** (role "Franchise Manager") see only their franchise.

## Add a franchise
Admin → **Franchises** → **New franchise**:
1. Franchise name, town, district, state.
2. **Localities** — tick the towns it runs, or **Add new locality** (search the place, set the radius in km). A locality belongs to one franchise only; attaching a switched-off locality switches it on.
3. **Manager** — full name, **mobile number (they sign in with it, OTP)**, email, date of birth, address, pincode, emergency contact.
4. **KYC** — Aadhaar number (checked, stored encrypted, shown as XXXX XXXX 1234), Aadhaar photo front (required) and back, PAN, GSTIN, business name.
5. **Agreement** — franchise share of RapidFix commission (%), start/end date, deposit, signed agreement upload.
6. **Payouts** — bank account, IFSC, UPI.

The mobile number must not belong to a technician or staff account. An existing customer number is upgraded.

## What belongs to a franchise
- **Bookings** made inside its localities (fixed at booking time).
- **Technicians** based in its localities (new sign-ups join automatically; head office can move any technician from the technician's page).
- **Customers** whose first booking was there (and anyone who booked there).
- Franchise bookings are offered only to that franchise's technicians (and head-office technicians).

## Franchise manager can
Dashboard, Bookings (assign/cancel), Customers, Technicians (verify documents), Complaints, Reports and downloads — all limited to their franchise. They cannot see other franchises, prices/services, payments, payouts, settings or staff.

## Reports
- Franchises page: this month per franchise, **Franchise report** for any dates (bookings, revenue, RapidFix commission, franchise share = commission × %), plus downloads.
- Downloads (CSV, opens in Excel): franchises, technicians, customers, addresses, bookings. Franchise managers get only their own.

## Suspend / end
Franchise page → **Suspend** or **End agreement**: the manager is signed out and can't sign in; new bookings in its localities go to head office. History is kept. **Activate** to restore.
